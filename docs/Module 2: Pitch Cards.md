# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.md) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**
Color Race, RoadRace, RoadWin.
**Player role and situation:**
The player will race with another through a coursewhile collecting colored peices, 3 of the same color to activate certain abilities.
**Repeated decision or action:**
Routes choices, collect colors, crashing, obstacles.
**Goal, pressure, or ending:**
Reach the finish line
**Hook:**
3 of the same color to collect what you need to win the race.
**Smallest useful physical prototype:**
Paper racetrack, car peices, colored tokens,, obstacles, dices for movements.
**Question I want listeners to answer:**
If it feels like a real racing game which is the goal. 
Does the color matching make it unique. There will also be a map.

Keep the cards as working notes. The workshop does not add a separate graded pitch submission.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**
Control the Outbreak, ZombieControl
**Player role and situation:**
The player is a survivor.  Its goal is to take back different locations that the zombies are taking over.
**Repeated decision or action:**
Fight zombies, decide which locations to win over first, stop the outbreak, not get bitten.
**Goal, pressure, or ending:**
Control the outbreak,  not let the whole town get infected. keeping track of how many humans are alive.
**Hook:**
The player can become a zombie and play in zombie mode.
**Smallest useful physical prototype:**
Paper map, paper locations, zombies.
**Question I want listeners to answer:**
Does this feel like a real zombie apocolpyse. 

Keep the cards as working notes. The workshop does not add a separate graded pitch submission.
