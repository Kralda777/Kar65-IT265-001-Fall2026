# IT265 Module 2: Idea Bank

List possible concepts before choosing one. Mark each as **ready to pitch**, **unclear loop**, **scope risk**, or **save for later**. Shortlist two or three ideas for the pitch round.

| Working title | Repeated player decision or action | Category | Reason or open question |

| RoadWinner | Choose routes, collect 3 of the same color, use advantages during the race | Racing| Racing concept clear goal to win 1st place. Ready to pitch.|

**Two or three concepts to pitch:**

1. ZombieTarget: **Unclear loop** The player is playing with other players: Against zombies, the goal is to win and survive the map, 
the zombies have the opportunity to win over certain location. For example food banks, hospitals, another unknown location. 
Their goal is to not let zombies take over the location and convert all the people.  
The hook would be that the player has the opportunity to get bitten and play as a zombie, it matters what location they choose to prioritize. Category is horror/ zombie. 
2. RoadWinner: This game is a first-person, multi-player game. The player will be racing against another car, have the opportunity while racing to collect certain "coins" 
These coins have the opportunity when you collect three in a row to speed up your car, fix your car ( fixing it after you crash), and one unknown advantage. The goal is to get three 
in a row and win the race. The player will be able to see the map and see what ways they would like to drive to get advantages and win the race which is the goal of the race. **Ready to pitch**
