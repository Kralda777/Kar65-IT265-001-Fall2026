# IT265 Module 2: Select and Scope a Concept
Compare the feedback for all your pitches before selecting one. Keep useful alternatives in your journal.

| Concept | Clear recurring decision? | Strongest appeal | Feasible first physical prototype? | Risk to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Racing Game| Choose routes, collect colors, decide on timing for use of abilities | Racing with color matching and different abilities to take the lead | Yes. Physical prototype would need a track, car pieces, dice, color pieces. | Track layout |
| Zombie Game | Decide which locations to prioritize and fight off zombies, survive. | Being able to play in zombie mode| Yes. Physical prototype would use a map, locations, zombie and player pieces| Uniqueness. |


**Selected concept and reason:**
I am choosing the racing game because after reviewing the inspiration being COD zombies my zombie game became too predictable
of a choice since it's a popular game that many have played.

**Feedback that changed or confirmed my choice:**
The instructor using COD zombies as an example confirmed my choice, and my peers mentioning that they would prefer my racing game over zombies.
**Other ideas to keep for later:**

- My zombie survival concept with survival and strategy mechanics.

**First physical prototype boundary:**

My first physical prototype will focus on the racing movement, competing against another player, and the color abilities. Players will use car pieces and roll two dice at the same time to move around the track.

**One feature to defer:**
Debating on being able to push or affect the cars near you, particularly in the physical prototype.

**One risk to test next:**
How many colors to have and how long the track will be if it's a circle track or street racing. The map is a risk and it's an important 
decision on the game. Basically the track layout.
