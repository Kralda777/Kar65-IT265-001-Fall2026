# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:**

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** Who is the player, what is the situation, and what problem matters?
The player is racing against another car, two player game. The player's goal is to finish the race first while choosing different routes and collecting colors.

2. **Experience and loop:** What should play feel like, and what does the player repeatedly decide or do?
I want the game to feel fast paced and arcade like. The player will race, collect colors which will give the opportunity for advantages, choose different routes, and once they get three of the same color they can use an ability.

3. **Goal and pressure:** What changes, what limits the player, and how does play end?
The player has to decide which routes and colors they want to go for while also dealing with the other cars and hazards. The game ends but will give 1st place and 2nd place.

4. **Scope:** What makes this version distinctive, and what can you test first with affordable physical materials?
What makes my game different is adding the color abilities and choices into a racing game. 
For the physical prototype I can test this with a track, car pieces, dice, colors, different routes and hazards.

## Treatment Draft
RoadRace is a first person racing game where the player races against other cars with the goal of finishing first. 
I want it to have the feeling of a racing simulator because the player will actually be driving the car, but I also want it to feel arcade like,
be simple and fast paced. The player will have different routes they can take throughout the race , with colors around the track that they can collect to help win the race.
The player needs to collect three of the same color before they can use that color's ability. Red can be used to bump another car, green can be used to repair or recover
your car, and yellow can give you a speed boost. This gives the player choices because they can decide if they want to take a certain route to get a color they need or focus on
getting ahead in the race. There can also be different shortcuts and hazards like water or obstacles that can affect the cars.
The main idea of the game is racing, collecting three matching colors, using abilities and deciding which routes to take to try to finish first. 
For my physical prototype I will use a track, car pieces, dice and color pieces. In the physical prototype everyone can roll two dice at the same time and move the amount they roll. 
If they roll doubles they can choose a color, and once they get three of the same color they can use the ability.
